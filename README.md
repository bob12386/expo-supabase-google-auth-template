## 📋 About This App

expo-supabase-google-auth-template is a ready-to-use mobile app template. It helps you add a simple and secure way for users to sign in with Google. You get built-in support for both Android and iOS devices. The app uses Supabase authentication with modern security practices, which means no complicated setup or localhost web servers are needed.

This template is based on Expo SDK 54 and uses features like PKCE (a security protocol), deep linking to handle sign-in smoothly, and safe session saving to keep users logged in safely on their phones. It is designed to work right away for anyone who wants a straightforward Google login method in their mobile app.

---

## 📱 Who Is This For

This template suits anyone wanting to build a mobile app with Google login capabilities. You do not need coding skills to download and run it. If you want a tested, secure way to sign users in on Android and iOS, this app is a good starting point.

## 💾 Download & Install

Once you have the file, you need to install the app on your device.

### Android Installation

1. Open the `.apk` file on your Android device.
2. If your phone asks for permission to install apps from unknown sources, allow it temporarily.
3. Follow the prompts to install.
4. Once installed, open the app from your home screen.

### iOS Installation

Installing `.ipa` files on iOS devices requires a few more steps because of Apple’s security rules:

1. Use a tool like Apple Configurator or third-party app installers (such as AltStore).
2. Connect your device to your computer.
3. Use these tools to sideload the `.ipa` file onto your iPhone or iPad.
4. After installation, if you see an “Untrusted Developer” message, go to:
   - **Settings > General > Device Management**
   - Find the profile and trust it.
5. Open the app from your home screen.

If you prefer, you can use TestFlight (Apple’s beta testing app) if available.

### Step 4: Start the App

Open the newly installed app. You should see the Google sign-in screen.

## 🔑 How to Use the App

1. When the app opens, tap the **Sign In with Google** button.
2. Pick your Google account or enter your credentials.
3. The app will securely log you in, using Supabase's authentication service.
4. After signing in, you will be taken to the main app screen.
5. Your session will stay active unless you log out.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting Tips

- **App Won't Install:** Make sure your device allows app installs from outside the app store.
- **Sign In Fails:** Check your internet connection. Make sure the Google account you use is valid.
- **App Crashes:** Try restarting your device and opening the app again.
- **Session Lost Too Often:** Ensure you have enough free storage and do not clear app data.
- **Deep Linking Issues:** Follow app prompts carefully. Sometimes, switching networks can cause delays.

If problems continue, visit the repository’s issues page or contact support if provided.
